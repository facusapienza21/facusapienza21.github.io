I am an Argentinian researcher.
I am currently on an extended period of travel with no formal affiliation, and in November I will be joining Université Grenoble Alpes (France) as a Chair of the [Multidisciplinary Institute in Artificial Intelligence](https://miai-cluster.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/miai-cluster/home-miai-1579890.kjsp?RH=6071747120751415).
My research lies in the intersection of novel data science methods and physics, with an emphasis on glacier modelling and scientific software development.
Before this, I completed my PhD at UC Berkeley under the supervision of Prof. Fernando Pérez (UC Berkeley, Project Jupyter) and Prof. Jonathan Taylor (Stanford), spent a year and a half as a postdoctoral researcher at the [Stanford Icy Physics Lab](https://icyphysics.stanford.edu/) working with Prof. Ching-Yao Lai, and was a visiting professor at the Department of Mathematics at the University of Buenos Aires.

A large part of my work is motivated by the use of differential equations as regression mechanisms to model complex geophysical systems.
By leveraging modern differential programming tools, it is possible to train machine learning models where the modelled response is given by the numerical solutions of physical constrained differential equations.
This allows the combination of both data and physics-driven approaches.
I focus on the applications of these tools for mountain glacier modelling, where I work in funcional inversion problems aiming to discover empirical laws governing the flow of ice.
With the advent of rich datasets with larger spatial and temporal coverage, it is becoming possible to model glaciers at global scale. 

I also work in data-driven approaches for the modelling of apparent polar wander paths, where the objective is to learn about the past motion of plate tectonics by relying on paleomagnetic data. 
This involves learning patterns from scarse and noise data supported in the sphere.

I am enthusiastic about open science and reproducibility research, and the ways that data science can help to democratize science. 
It is my belief that physics and earth science will benefit from the incorporation of data driven-approaces.

🌱 I will soon be opening PhD and postdoc positions in my group at Grenoble — feel free to reach out if you are interested!
