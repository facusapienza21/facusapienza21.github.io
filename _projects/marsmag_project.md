As part of my PhD program at UC Berkeley, I engaged in a two-year collaboration with [Abigail Azari](https://abbyazari.github.io/), [Fernando Pérez](https://bids.berkeley.edu/people/fernando-perez) and colleagues to bring statistical and machine learning methods to the analysis of data from Mars.

Mars has no global magnetic field, so the solar wind interacts directly with its upper atmosphere and creates an induced magnetosphere.
Using data from NASA's MAVEN mission, we studied how the interplanetary magnetic field drapes around the planet.
We also built a virtual solar wind monitor at Mars, based on Gaussian processes, that estimates upstream solar wind conditions together with their uncertainty.
