Geomagnetism

Modelling variations of Earth's magnetic field with physics-informed ML and SDEs

This is a new project, more to come!

Earth’s magnetic field is generated by the motion of liquid iron in the outer core, and it varies on a huge range of time scales: from years and decades in observatory and satellite records, to thousands and millions of years in paleomagnetic records, including full reversals of the field’s polarity.

The goal of this project is to model these variations at different scales by combining physics-informed machine learning with stochastic differential equation (SDE) modelling. SDEs provide a natural framework to describe the evolution of the field as the combination of deterministic dynamics and random fluctuations, while machine learning lets us learn the parts of these dynamics that are not well constrained by theory directly from data.

This work builds on our recent analysis of ordinal pattern statistics in paleomagnetic records, where we asked whether the evolution of Earth’s dipole is better explained by stochastic or chaotic dynamics.

  1. Noise Effects on Ordinal Pattern Statistics via Majorization
    Facundo Sapienza
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.22463, Aug 2026
    Abs Bib HTML PDF Code

    The research addresses how observational noise distorts ordinal pattern probability distributions in time series analysis, particularly affecting the Bandt-Pompe permutation entropy framework and complexity-entropy causality plane. This distortion can incorrectly classify dynamical systems, especially problematic for geophysical signals with poor sampling and low signal-to-noise ratios. Using majorization theory, the author characterizes these statistical distortions and develops corrective methods to restore classification accuracy. The work introduces techniques to characterize noisy dynamical series and quantify measurement noise without fitting procedures. As an application, the study analyzes paleomagnetic records to assess whether Earth’s dipole evolution is better explained by stochastic or chaotic dynamics.
